## The challenge
SteelFab had grown through several acquisitions into a coast-to-coast provider of steel, but its website hadn't kept up. A visitor couldn't tell how far the company reached, which plant or office served their region, or how to start a project.

Three generations of family ownership had built a solid brand. The site needed to carry it, not shrink it.

## What we did
This was a total overhaul, and it started with the words. New messaging explains the national footprint plainly and walks a prospect through how a project begins, from first call to delivery.

The page layouts were designed around that copy, so the words and the visuals make one argument instead of two. Elements of the SteelFab logo became the design language, so the rebrand still feels like SteelFab, only bigger.

A locations page with a map puts every plant, office, and regional provider one click from the homepage, each with the right person to contact.

## The results
SteelFab now presents as the national provider it is. Regional offices, contacts, and the full breadth of services are easy to find, and the company reads as one company from coast to coast. Lisa put it simply: they explained their goals, and we exceeded them.
