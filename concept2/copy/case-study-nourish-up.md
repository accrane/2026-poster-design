## The challenge
Two organizations were becoming one. That meant a new name, a new brand, and two websites full of programs, history, and heart that now had to live together on one site.

Three very different people needed to find what they came for in seconds: a neighbor looking for food assistance, a volunteer looking for a shift, and a donor looking for a way to give. And the launch date was set before the design was.

## What we did
We started with the map, not the paint. Every page from both old sites was sorted into one structure built around those three visitors, so the homepage sends each of them to the right place in one click.

The new brand's colors carry the whole site. A video reel on the homepage says in a few seconds what the old sites needed paragraphs for. On the interior pages, the heaviest content is sorted into visual tiles, so a long list of programs reads as a quick choice instead of a wall of text.

We built it on WordPress, hand-coded and easy to update, so the Nourish Up team can keep it current without calling us. Then we kept the schedule honest and launched on the date they had circled.

## The results
Two sites became one. The new site reflects the new name and mission, and it makes the three big jobs simpler: get help, volunteer, give. It launched on deadline, and the team owns a site they can run themselves.
