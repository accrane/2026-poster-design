## The challenge
The Whitewater Center is home to the world's largest man-made whitewater river, and it's open all year. Hours shift with the season and the weather. Activities run on schedules that change from one day to the next. Festivals, races, camps, lessons, dining, and private events each bring their own crowd with their own questions.

For most guests, the website is the plan: check today's hours, see what's running, buy a pass, go. A brochure site can't keep up with that. The Center needed a site its own team could update in minutes, many times a day, without calling anyone.

## What we did
We built whitewater.org as a living system, not a stack of pages. Today's hours and the open-or-closed status of each location sit at the top of every visit, edited by staff on the spot.

Activities, schedules, passes, camps, lessons, dining, festivals, races, jobs, and event spaces are each their own kind of content with their own filters, so the team adds a new camp or a festival day the way they'd fill out a form. A Plan Your Visit path pulls together what a first-timer needs. Registration status, custom notices, and show-or-hide switches on every schedule mean a rained-out session or a sold-out class is one toggle, not a phone call.

Over the years the partnership has grown past the main site: an event-planning portal for booked clients that talks to their CRM, an interactive map, a data dashboard, and tools around their ticketing system.

## The results
The website is how the Whitewater Center talks to the public every day. Guests plan around it: hours, conditions, what's on, what to buy. The team runs it themselves, in real time, and when the Center adds something new, the site already has a place for it. Years in, we still know their brand, their pain points, and what's coming next.
